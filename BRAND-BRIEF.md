## Storage & serving instructions (real)
- Keep frozen — up to 5 months from packaging date, best used before then.
- Thaw in the refrigerator for at least 24 hours before serving.
- Serve chilled, or warm slightly in the microwave 30–40 seconds (varies by microwave). Dogs often prefer it served warm.
- Once thawed, keep refrigerated and use within 5 days.

## Feeding guide (per meal, real)
- Small dogs (up to 10kg): 150–250g
- Medium dogs (10–25kg): 250–450g
- Large dogs (25kg+): 450–650g
- Always ensure fresh drinking water is available.

## Optional boosts (real)
- Extra protein: add 1–2 cooked eggs (however your dog prefers), served alongside the meal.
- Extra calcium: add 1–2 tablespoons of all-natural plain yoghurt.
